How to fill in:
- Edit, add or delete activity blocks freely. **Copy a block to add one, delete a block to remove one.**
- To add a day, copy a whole `## Day` section. Dates follow the start date automatically.
- **Time**: 24-hour, e.g. `13:30`

- **Closed on**: e.g. `Tue` (only if the place has a day off)
- Photos you already added stay as they are.

# Tips (one per line)
- Use the Grab app for rides — cheap and easy everywhere on the island.
- Hot and humid with sudden rain: light clothes and a small umbrella.
- Many hawker stalls close one day a week — check before you go.
- Cover shoulders and knees when visiting temples and mosques.
- Carry small cash; most hawkers don't take cards.

# Packing list (one per line)
- Passport / IC
- Light breathable clothes
- Small umbrella
- Sunscreen & hat
- Comfy sandals
- Mosquito repellent
- Power bank
- Small cash for hawkers
